Write a compelling synopsis for this novel.

The author's story and explicit instructions are authoritative. Preserve the story's actual premise, central conflict, major events, important character actions, major revelations, turning points, and consequences.

Do not soften, euphemize, generalize, omit, replace, or reinterpret concrete story events merely to make the synopsis sound more elegant. If an important event is explicitly established — including violence, death, betrayal, destruction, sexual content, a major revelation, or another consequential action — preserve the event and its narrative consequence clearly.

A synopsis may condense scenes and details, but it must not erase events that are essential to understanding what actually happens in the story.

Use established character names whenever they exist. Only create a new character name when a genuinely new character must be identified and no established name exists.

Focus on:

* The main premise
* The central conflict
* The protagonist's major goal and obstacles
* The major turning points
* Important character choices and consequences
* The major progression and resolution of the story
* The emotional core of the story

Do not write a chapter-by-chapter outline.

Do not invent major events, characters, revelations, motivations, or outcomes that are not supported by the story context or the author's instructions.

Write the synopsis as continuous prose in 5-10 paragraphs.

Return only the synopsis text.
