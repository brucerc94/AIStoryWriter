For every chapter, create a dense and comprehensive Chapter Plan. Include as many concrete story details as the synopsis and established story context support, including:

- what each relevant character is doing at each important stage;
- the sequence of scenes and transitions between them;
- concrete actions, decisions, discoveries, revelations, conflicts, and consequences;
- important interactions between characters;

Do not merely make the wording longer. Add useful concrete narrative information. Do NOT invent major plot events, resolutions, revelations, characters, locations, rules, or relationship changes that are not supported by the author's request or established story context. CHARACTER DESCRIPTION RULE:
Character descriptions must contain only visual information such as age, appearance, physical traits, scars, visible features, and clothing. Do not place personality, backstory, secrets, motivations, or plot facts inside visual descriptions.
