Generate the complete outline for this novel using the chapter-by-chapter rich treatment pipeline.

The generator first partitions the author's Synopsis into chronological chapter source blocks. Each source block is then developed independently into a rich narrative treatment using the author's full Creative Intent and Writing Style, relevant established Characters, relevant World, and the previous chapter's context.

The outline is not a final novel chapter and it is not a terse checklist. Each chapter treatment should preserve the source material while developing it into concrete, chronological scenes that identify what characters do, say, notice, feel, decide, and respond to; how the setting and atmosphere function; how transitions and consequences connect; and what representative dialogue belongs in the scene when useful.

AUTHOR PROFILE
Apply both Creative Intent and Writing Style directly to the treatment. The profile controls narrative emphasis, point of view, pacing, descriptive and sensory density, dialogue style and frequency, emotional focus, genre feel, romance/violence treatment, and target level of detail.

SOURCE FIDELITY
Preserve the chapter source block's events, order, causal relationships, meaningful character interactions, discoveries, decisions, and consequences. Do not replace the source with a generic summary. Do not invent new major plot events, characters, relationships, revelations, twists, or outcomes.

CREATIVE EXPANSION
The treatment may expand established events with scene-level description, emotional texture, connective actions, and representative dialogue so that Write Chapter can turn it into polished prose without having to invent the core story. Representative dialogue is allowed when the source does not provide exact wording, but it must remain faithful to the established situation and character intent.

OUTPUT
Each chapter must use only its chapter heading followed by the rich narrative treatment. Do not use Chapter Plan, numbered checklist items, bullet lists, extra planning sections, or a separate Continuity section.

Generate exactly the requested number of chapters in chronological order and preserve the full story arc across them.
